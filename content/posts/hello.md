This is the first note.

I am keeping it small on purpose. A personal blog does not need to arrive fully formed. It needs a place to put thoughts before they harden into opinions.

The useful pattern is probably this:

- write the note while the idea is still warm
- link it to whatever it touches
- come back later and make it clearer

This post lives at `content/posts/hello.md`. New posts can sit beside it.
