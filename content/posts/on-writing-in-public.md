Writing in public changes the shape of a thought.

In a private note, an idea can stay vague for a long time. That is useful sometimes. Not every thought is ready to be tested. But when something is written for another person, even a hypothetical reader, the loose parts become easier to see.

That does not mean every post needs to be a finished essay. I want this blog to leave room for working notes: small observations, half-settled opinions, and practical things I might want to find again later.

The standard is not perfection. The standard is whether the note says something specific enough to be useful.
