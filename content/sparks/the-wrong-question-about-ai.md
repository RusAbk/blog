>[!note] Everyone asks
>"Will AI replace my job?"

>[!warning] Almost no one asks
>"Which parts of my job were never real value?"

AI does not just automate work.

It makes visible which work was always just process, ritual, and the appearance of effort.

That is the scarier question.

And the more useful one.
